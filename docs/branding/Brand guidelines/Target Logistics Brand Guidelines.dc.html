05 — Patterns

Brand Patterns

Two signature patterns extend the identity across packaging, environments and digital surfaces. Use the Doodle Pattern on light grounds and the Triangle Cluster — a royal-blue field with darker-blue triangles — on dark.

Doodle Pattern — Light
Memphis scatter of brand shapes, indigo at 12% on white. Default surface texture for light screens, packaging and documents. target-pattern.svg
Triangle Cluster — Dark
Royal-blue field with darker-blue triangles. For dark surfaces, hero panels and vehicle livery.
